Exhibit A

Post-traumatic anosmia is a real, measurable injury.

Loss of smell after head trauma is easy to dismiss on paper and hard to live with in practice. It carries clinical risk, safety risk, and financial consequence — all of which can be documented.

Clinical impact

  • Increased risk of depression (6–8×)
  • Safety risks — gas, smoke, spoiled food go undetected
  • Loss of taste and its social impact
  • Reduced earning capacity in smell-dependent work

Objective methodology

  • Standardized olfactory testing (UPSIT)
  • Full ENT examination
  • Injury correlation analysis
  • Functional & quality-of-life assessment
